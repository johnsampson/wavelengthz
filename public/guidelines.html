Community Guidelines

Version 1.1 — Effective September 3, 2026 · Terms · Privacy · Safety · Cookies · DMCA

A violation of these guidelines is a violation of our Terms of Service, and can lead to content removal, message/photo restriction, or account suspension.

Wavelengthz exists to connect real people over real musical taste. These guidelines exist to keep it that way.

Be a real person

  • Use your own name, your own photos, and be honest about who you are. Impersonating someone else — a real person, a celebrity, or a fictional persona — isn't allowed.
  • Don't create more than one account, and don't create an account on someone else's behalf.
  • Don't use bots, scrapers, or other automated tools against the app.

Respect other people

  • No harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
  • No unwanted sexual content or advances. If someone tells you to stop, stop.
  • No doxxing — don't share another person's private information (address, workplace, phone number, etc.) without their consent, on or off the app.
  • Disagreements happen. Cruelty doesn't need to.

Keep content appropriate

  • No nudity or sexually explicit photos in your profile.
  • No depictions of minors, ever, in any context.
  • No graphic violence, gore, or content promoting self-harm.
  • No content that's illegal where you or the person you're matched with lives.

Uploaded photos are automatically screened before anyone else sees them — see our Privacy Policy §2 and Terms of Service §4.

No solicitation, spam, or scams

  • Don't use Wavelengthz to advertise a business, service, or commercial product — including your own OnlyFans, crypto, MLM, or "sugar" arrangement.
  • Don't solicit or offer commercial sexual services.
  • Watch for and never participate in romance scams: asking a match for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or financial information is never legitimate, no matter how convincing the story. Report it immediately — see our Safety Tips.
  • Don't send unsolicited bulk messages or use the app to build a marketing list.

Meeting in person

Wavelengthz can't verify anyone's identity or intentions beyond what they tell us. Before you meet someone from the app, read our Safety Tips — video chat first, meet in public, tell a friend your plans, and trust your instincts.

Reporting and enforcement

If someone violates these guidelines, block them (removes them from your view and theirs, immediately, no questions asked) and report them (flags their account for review — see Terms of Service §6 for how that works).

Violations can result in, depending on severity: a warning, removal of the specific content, temporary restriction, or permanent account termination. We may act on a single severe violation without a warning first — this includes anything involving a minor, a credible threat of violence, or a financial scam.

If you believe your account was actioned in error, contact us at connect@wavelengthz.com.
